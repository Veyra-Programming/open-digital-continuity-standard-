Standards Governance & Royalty-Free IP Policy

OPEN STANDARDS TRACK

1. Architecture Committee Charter

The Open Digital Continuity Standard (ODCS) is governed by the Open Digital Continuity Architecture Committee, chaired by Lead Architect Anirudha Puthraya. The Committee ensures architectural coherence, backward compatibility across schema migrations, and language-level bindings.

2. Specification Maturity Stages

  1. Stage 0 • Exploratory Draft: Working group concepts defining continuity invariants and mathematical problem statements.
  2. Stage 1 • Committee Draft / RFC: Formally structured specifications published on https://odcs.in/spec/ for public commentary and debate.
  3. Stage 2 • Candidate Recommendation: Validated against at least two independent, interoperable implementations across desktop, edge, and browser substrates.
  4. Stage 3 • Canonical ODCS Standard: Permanently ratified standard. Modifications require formal RFC proposals and backward compatibility bridges.

3. Royalty-Free (FRAND-RF) Patent Commitment

In alignment with the W3C Patent Policy and IETF RFC 3979 standards, all members and contributors to the ODCS specifications make an irrevocable commitment:

Royalty-Free Pledge: Every contributor grants an irrevocable, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to practice any patent claims essential to implementing the normative requirements of the Open Digital Continuity Standard.
