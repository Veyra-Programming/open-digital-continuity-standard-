ODCS-LEGAL-001 • CANONICAL TRACK

Privacy Policy & Local Data Sovereignty

ZERO TRACKING VERIFIED
The Sovereign Privacy Invariant: Under ODCS, local non-volatile storage is the private authority of record. We do not track, profile, monetize, or harvest user data. Your compute substrate belongs to you.

1. Architectural Principle: Zero Non-Consensual Telemetry

Contemporary web software routinely deploys surveillance scripts, session replays, and invasive cross-origin analytics. The Open Digital Continuity Standard Working Group categorically rejects these practices.

  • No Personal Data Harvesting: We do not log client IP addresses, device identifiers, biometric information, or telemetry without your active, opt-in consent.
  • In-Browser Execution Sovereignty: Code executed in the Haven Web Playground runs 100% locally in your browser's client-side V8/WebAssembly thread. Nothing you type is transmitted to a cloud telemetry server.
  • Zero Cross-Site Trackers: We use no tracking pixels, advertising networks, or third-party behavioral trackers.

2. Local Storage, Service Workers & Offline Caching

To deliver offline digital continuity, the ODCS web portal uses standard client-side browser storage strictly for functional operational performance:

  • Service Worker Cache (sw.js): Stores static assets (CSS, SVGs, specifications) so the site remains operational during subway tunnel transit, high-altitude flights, and rural network airgaps.
  • Client Memory & IndexedDB: The interactive continuity simulator stages mutation journals and CRDT outbox buffers purely within your browser sandbox.
  • Visual Theme Preference: Stores your dark mode visual preference in localStorage.

3. Layer 3 Edge Network Telemetry

The ODCS network telemetry probe (/api/telemetry/probe) operated on Cloudflare Edge provides client applications with round-trip-time (RTT) latency measurements. This probe is completely stateless, performs zero database logging, and does not retain IP addresses or headers beyond the instantaneous TCP socket lifecycle.

4. Global Regulatory Compliance

Our strict data minimization and local sovereign architecture ensure full compliance with international privacy mandates:

  • GDPR (EU 2016/679): Compliance by design (Article 25) and strict data minimization (Article 5(1)(c)).
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023): Zero unauthorized processing of digital personal data.

5. Contact & Governance Custodian

For privacy inquiries or cryptographic verification audits, contact the Architecture Committee at privacy@odcs.in or Lead Architect Anirudha Puthraya.

ODCS-LEGAL-003 • CRITICAL SYSTEMS

Security Architecture & Vulnerability Disclosure

COORDINATED DISCLOSURE

Digital continuity is mission-critical infrastructure. ODCS is designed to protect medical records in ambulances, banking ledgers during partition, and aerospace telemetry. Security and cryptographic integrity are paramount.

1. In-Scope Security Components

  • Cryptographic Chain Integrity: SHA-256 state digests, field-level binary Merkle trees, and RFC 8785 JSON canonicalization.
  • CRDT Consensus Robustness: Resistance to state poisoning, causal frontier violations, and outbox replay thundering herds.
  • Haven C VM & Bytecode Sandbox: Memory safety in the grammar parser, opcode dispatch engine, and durable record immutability enforcement.
  • Cloudflare Edge Continuity Gateway: Telemetry probing and sync endpoint verification.

2. Coordinated Disclosure SLA

We commit to the following response timeline for responsible security researchers:

  • Initial Acknowledgement: Within 24 hours of submission.
  • Triage & Severity Assessment: Within 72 hours.
  • Remediation Target: Critical vulnerabilities patched within 14 days; public disclosure coordinated following downstream release.

3. Safe Harbor Guarantee

If you conduct vulnerability research in good faith and adhere to these guidelines, we will consider your research authorized, will not pursue civil litigation, and will not report your activities to law enforcement.

Security Contact: Please send encrypted reports to security@odcs.in. Include proof-of-concept steps and environmental details.
ODCS-LEGAL-004 • GOVERNANCE CHARTER

Standards Governance & Royalty-Free IP Policy

ROYALTY-FREE COMMITMENT

1. The Architecture Committee

The Open Digital Continuity Standard is stewarded by the Open Digital Continuity Architecture Committee, chaired by Lead Architect Anirudha Puthraya. The Committee coordinates working drafts, schema consensus, RFC reviews, and language interoperability.

2. Specification Maturity Lifecycle

  1. Working Draft (WD): Exploratory architectural proposals detailing invariants and candidate interfaces.
  2. Committee Draft / Request for Comments (RFC): Formally reviewed specifications published on odcs.in/spec/ for public review.
  3. Candidate Recommendation (CR): Verified across at least two independent production implementations (e.g. Haven C-VM and browser reference clients).
  4. Canonical Standard (ODCS): Ratified, permanent reference standard. Backward-incompatible modifications require a major version bump.

3. Royalty-Free (FRAND-RF) Patent Commitment

To safeguard implementors against patent holdup and submarine litigation, all contributors to the ODCS specification irrevocably grant to any implementor a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to practice any patent claims essential to implementing the standard.
