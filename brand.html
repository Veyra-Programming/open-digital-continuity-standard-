Brand Identity & Conformance Marks

ASSETS & TOKENS

The names "Open Digital Continuity Standard", "ODCS", "Haven Language", and "Heaven Language" signify mathematical durability, tamper evidence, and interruption-tolerant computing. These guidelines ensure consistent, high-trust presentation across the ecosystem.

1. Conformance Certification Badges

Applications and edge nodes satisfying ODCS verification criteria are permitted to display the official compliance badges:

✓ ODCS-1.0.0 CERTIFIED
✓ ZERO INTENT LOSS (ZIL) COMPLIANT
✓ HAVEN NATIVE RUNTIME 1.0.0
